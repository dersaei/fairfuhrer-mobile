# Message to Miriam — B.2 open questions

**Purpose:** Get her sign-off on the few real product decisions for pin gating. Business-level only, no technical wording.

---

Hi Miriam,

Before we start the next release (pin submissions from the mobile app), I have just a few decisions to check with you. Everything else I'll figure out on my side.

## 1. Can a Partner add several pins?

If a Partner runs a bigger business — for example, a hotel with a restaurant and a spa — should their yearly subscription cover **multiple pins**, or is it one pin per Partner?

- **Multiple pins included** — makes sense for bigger businesses; the tier price (50 / 100 / 200 €) already reflects the size *(my recommendation)*
- **One pin per Partner** — each additional pin costs extra
- **Something in between** — e.g., small business up to 2 pins, medium up to 5

## 2. What happens when a Partner's subscription expires?

When a Partner's yearly subscription ends and they don't renew, what should happen to their pin?

- **7-day grace period** — pin stays visible with a small "Renewal due" notice; then hidden if not renewed *(my recommendation, gives them a chance)*
- **Immediate** — pin hidden the moment subscription ends
- **30-day grace** — longer window

## 3. Should Reisender+ suggestions get priority?

When a paying Reisender+ (Fairführer+) suggests a new commercial place, should their suggestion appear **first in your review queue**? A small perk for paying users.

- **Yes** — Reisender+ suggestions marked as priority for editorial review *(my recommendation)*
- **No** — treat all suggestions equally

---

## What the release will do (for your awareness — no action needed):

1. **Reisenders can add Sehenswertes pins directly from the mobile app** — full form, same as Partners have on web. Free, unlimited.
2. **Reisenders can suggest commercial places** (restaurants, shops, etc.) via a simpler form. You review before publishing.
3. **Partners can create their commercial pins from mobile** — same form and flow they already have on web. Nothing new for you to manage.
4. **Partners see their own pin(s) in the "Profil" tab** with a status (active / pending / expired).

Timeline: ~1-2 weeks once we settle Q1-Q3.
